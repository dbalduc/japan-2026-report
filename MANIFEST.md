# Asset manifest — Japan 2026

Every photo, audio and video slot the site is expecting, in page order.
Drop files into `images/`, `audio/` and `video/` using these **exact filenames**
(lowercase, hyphens, `.jpg`). A slot with no file shows a labelled dashed box
instead — nothing ever renders broken, so ship early and fill in over time.

Check progress any time by loading the site with `?todo` on the URL and opening
the browser console — it prints exactly what is still missing.

## Day 5 · Tuesday, July 21 · The Kerama Islands
*THE KERAMA DIVE*

- `images/d5-01-kerama-blue.jpg` **(full-bleed)** — 11:21, between dives. The water out here has its own name — Kerama Blue. The park was designated in 2014 and holds 248 coral species, about 62% of everything Japan has.
- `images/d5-04-dive-slate.jpg` **(full-bleed)** — Sam’s slate. All three sites, entry and exit times, max and average depth, viz, temp, and a highlights column that ends — bottom right, underlined, with a doodle — MANTA!
